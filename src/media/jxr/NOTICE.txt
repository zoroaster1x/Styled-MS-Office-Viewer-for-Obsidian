Bundled JPEG XR decoder
=======================

src/media/jxr/jxr_dec.js and src/media/jxr/jxr_dec.wasm are the decoder from
@jsquash/jxr (packaged here as @discourse/jxr 1.0.0), a WebAssembly build of
the jxrlib JPEG XR codec.
